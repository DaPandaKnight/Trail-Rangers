Important safety information

Before you plan a route

RidgeWalker generates suggested routes using an automated terrain-cost model based on elevation and slope data.

Routes are algorithmically estimated and have not been verified against real trail conditions, track markers, river crossings, avalanche risk, seasonal closures, land access restrictions, or on-the-ground hazards.

This is not a substitute for proper trip planning, a topographic map, and sound backcountry judgement.

Use of any route generated by this site is entirely at your own risk.
